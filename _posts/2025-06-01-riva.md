Riva Jangra has joined us for the summer! Riva is a visiting researcher from the Perimeter Institute who will begin her PhD in physics at Dartmouth College in Fall 2025. Her past experience includes helping build the atomic-level compiler infrastructure for Professor Rajibul Islam’s trapped-ion quantum computer, exploring AI applications in Celestial Holography with Professor Sabrina Pasterski, and most recently working on topological graph theoretic applications for superconducting qubits with Professor Andrew Houck’s lab. Riva will be working on developing and building SLM architecture and coding Spectrum AWGs for tweezer rearrangement. We're excited to have her here!
